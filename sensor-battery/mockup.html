Karoo 3 extension · design concepts v2

Sensor Juice

Battery overview for every paired sensor, used mainly before and after the ride. Screens are drawn at real Karoo 3 size (480×800) in dark and light ride modes. Low batteries come first and get large cards; healthy ones fold into one compact block. Sensors the Karoo only reports as a level (New, Good, OK, Low, Critical) get a 5-segment gauge. Sensors with a real percentage get a continuous bar and the number. All values are example data.

Hero · app screen · 480×800 · opened from the Karoo menu or the pre-ride notification

Before the ride: battery check

The verdict line answers "can I ride?" first. Each attention card states what to do, and what the estimate is based on. The healthy group shows a gauge and name only.

Hero · app screen · 480×800 · opened from the ride-end notification

After the ride: what to charge tonight

Grouped by when to charge. Each device shows how much it dropped during this ride; the drain rates feed the "rides left" estimates.

Triggers · SDK effects

How the overview reaches the rider

A ride-end SystemNotification carries an actionIntent that opens the post-ride screen. An InRideAlert fires when recording starts with something below 15 %, and when a device first drops a band during the ride. In-ride alerts cannot open an app.

Secondary · in-ride data fields

Compact field: weakest battery only

A 1×1 field that stays calm until something drops below 30 %, then shows that device. A slim strip variant can sit at the bottom of a page and stays blank while everything is fine.

Colour scale

Four bands, each with its own icon

Each band has an icon, a word and a gauge length, so it reads without colour and in direct sun. Coarse levels map onto the same bands, choosing the worse band when a level spans two.

Green starts at 50 % rather than 60 %. With a 60 % line the OK level (46–80 %) would sit across two bands, and most healthy sensors would show yellow. Coin-cell devices (HR strap, Di2 shifters) say "Replace CR2032/CR1632" instead of "Charge".
